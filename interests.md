# Interests

Things I find genuinely interesting — topics I read about, problems I tinker on, and threads I keep pulling on.

## Agentic AI & LLM Systems

I'm fascinated by what happens when language models stop being chat assistants and start being systems that *act* — multi-agent orchestration, memory and context design, evaluation harnesses, guardrails, and the messy engineering gap between a demo agent and a production one. A lot of my day job lives here, and I still find it interesting outside of work.

## Edge & Spatial Computing

There's something compelling about getting ML to run on hardware that can't phone home — constrained devices, real-time pipelines, latency budgets measured in milliseconds. Mixed reality and computer vision on edge nodes were my entry point; the intersection with modern agentic systems is where I think the next interesting problems are.

## Knowledge Representation & Retrieval

OWL/RDF ontologies and knowledge graphs as a complement to vector search, not a replacement. Hybrid retrieval that actually reduces hallucinations rather than just claiming to. The question of how you encode domain jargon and compliance logic in a form an LLM can reason over.

## Data Engineering & Lakehouses

Medallion architectures, lineage, feature stores, experiment-to-production loops — the infrastructure that makes ML reliable rather than just impressive in a notebook.

## Simulation & Decision Modelling

My academic background sits in systems modelling and multi-criteria decision analysis. I keep coming back to that lens when thinking about complex trade-offs — resource allocation, operational bottlenecks, ranking under uncertainty.
